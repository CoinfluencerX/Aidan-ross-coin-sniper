### (c) Rockstar, Take-Two and Cfx.re (FiveM) policy on crypto

- **Rockstar RP-server policy, 2022-11-18** (support.rockstargames.com/articles/11374598771091).
  - Enforcement priorities include "commercial exploitation, including the sale of 'loot boxes'… the sale of virtual currencies… or the use of cryptocurrencies or crypto assets (e.g. 'NFTs')".
  - "This is not a license…"
  - Lil Durk's NFT server was shut down around then.
- **Cfx.re Creator Platform License Agreement, "Last Updated: Sept. 10 2026"** (fivem.net/terms redirects to this PDF). The agreement is with Rockstar and Take-Two. §3.1 prohibits:
  - **(1)** any mechanic that lets users put in "Real Money" (explicitly including crypto) and then "Cash Out", including real-money gambling.
  - **(3)** selling virtual currency for Real Money.
  - **(5)** running a server as the "official" server of a brand or sponsor.
  - **(7)** third-party sponsorships in Creator Content.
  - **(8)** "facilitating the use, sale, or promotion of cryptocurrencies or crypto assets (e.g. 'NFTs,' 'tokens' or 'meme coins')."
  - Exceptions need separate express written terms from Rockstar.
  - **Adin's described design (a server coin plus cash-out) conflicts directly with (1) and (8).** The agreement does not yet name GTA VI as a covered game.
- **2026 enforcement.** Take-Two had RAGE:MP shut down: the announcement was around May 2026, the server list was wiped 2026-06-01, and full shutdown could come as early as 2026-08-31. RAGE:MP's own announcement said "FiveM is the only authorized platform for GTA V multiplayer modding" (Notebookcheck, 2026-05-29). alt:V reportedly followed (UNVERIFIED).
- **No crypto statement for GTA VI.** I found no 2026 Rockstar or Take-Two statement specifically about crypto in GTA VI. Rockstar's 2026-08-26 statement concerned leaks. A Rockstar X post saying there is "no early access program" is cited only secondhand (MEXC; UNVERIFIED).
